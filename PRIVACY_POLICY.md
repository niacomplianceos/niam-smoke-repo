# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 March 2026_

Acme Retail Private Limited ("we", "us") operates the Acme mobile
application and website. This policy explains what personal data we
collect and how we use it.

## 1. Data we collect

When you create an account or use our services, we collect:

- **Email address** — to create your account, send transactional
  notifications, and respond to support requests.
- **Phone number** — for order updates and delivery coordination.
- **Profile information** — the display name and preferences you provide
  in your account settings.
- **Account identifier** — an internal user ID we generate to associate
  your activity with your account.
- **IP address** — recorded automatically in our server logs for security
  and fraud prevention.

## 2. How we use your data

We use the data above to provide and improve the service, to communicate
with you about your orders, and to meet our legal obligations.

## 3. Sharing

We may share data with service providers who help us operate the
platform. We require these providers to handle data only on our
instructions.

## 4. Retention

We keep personal data for as long as your account is active, and for a
reasonable period afterwards where required by law.

## 5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal
data by writing to privacy@acme.example.

## 6. Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@acme.example
